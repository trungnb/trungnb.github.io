I am a dentist and researcher working on CBCT-based oral radiology, 3D dental imaging, and quantitative analysis. I hold a **Master of Science in Odonto-Stomatology** from the University of Medicine and Pharmacy at Ho Chi Minh City (UMP HCMC) and a **Doctor of Medicine in Odonto-Stomatology** from the University of Medicine and Pharmacy, Hue University. My clinical experience in oral surgery and implantology informs this work, including published studies of upper-airway dimensions and maxillary premolar alveolar-bone morphology.

### 🔬 Research Focus
* **CBCT morphometrics:** upper-airway, maxillary premolar, crown-root ratio, and CEJ measurements.
* **Quantitative research:** reproducible data cleaning, analysis, and visualisation in R and Python.
* **Exploratory imaging and AI:** TotalSegmentator, NiBabel, synthetic tabular data, and multimodal AI.
* **Educational software:** [PeriApicaI](https://periapical.ai.studio), an experimental, non-clinical prototype for periapical-radiograph review, developed with AI assistance.
