I am a dentist and researcher working on **CBCT, 3D dental imaging, and quantitative analysis**. I hold an MSc in Odonto-Stomatology from the University of Medicine and Pharmacy at Ho Chi Minh City and an MD in Odonto-Stomatology from Hue University. My clinical background in oral surgery and implantology informs research on image-based anatomy and measurement.

### Research focus
* **CBCT and 3D morphometrics:** upper-airway and dentoalveolar anatomy, with exploratory crown–root transition analysis.
* **Quantitative methods:** reproducible data cleaning, statistical analysis, and visualisation in R and Python.
* **Exploratory AI and software:** 3D imaging pipelines, synthetic tabular data, and [PeriApicaI](https://periapical.ai.studio), an experimental non-clinical educational prototype.

### Recent updates
* **2026:** Released PeriApicaI and received [Silver Tier (Top 500), AI Riser Vietnam](/files/AI_Riser_Vietnam_2026_Top_500_Nguyen_Bao_Trung.pdf).
* **December 2025:** Completed an MSc thesis on CBCT upper-airway analysis in obstructive sleep apnoea.
* **2025:** Published first-author and co-authored CBCT studies in *Vietnam Medical Journal* ([airway](https://doi.org/10.51298/vmj.v553i3.15486); [premolar bone](https://doi.org/10.51298/vmj.v555i3.16172)).
