### Abstract
This study assessed the anatomical features of the alveolar bone in the maxillary premolar region in young Vietnamese adults using CBCT. Findings revealed a frequently thin buccal bone plate (< 2mm) and close root proximity to the maxillary sinus, concluding that these anatomical variations pose suboptimal conditions for immediate implant placement and require careful preoperative planning.
