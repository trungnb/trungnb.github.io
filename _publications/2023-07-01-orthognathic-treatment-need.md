### Abstract
This hospital-level cross-sectional study examined sagittal skeletal relationships and functional need for orthognathic surgery among 120 adult patients with occlusal problems. The reported sample included Class I, II, and III sagittal relationships and used the Index of Orthognathic Functional Treatment Need (IOFTN) to describe treatment need.
