### Tóm tắt
This study investigated the volumetric and cross-sectional morphometry of the upper airway in Vietnamese adults with and without Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA) using Cone-Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT). 

### Đóng góp (85% - Principal Author)
- Formulated overall study conceptualization and anatomical measurement protocol.
- Performed 3D volumetric airway segmentation and statistical modeling in R and Python.
- Authored the full manuscript and coordinated peer-review revisions.
