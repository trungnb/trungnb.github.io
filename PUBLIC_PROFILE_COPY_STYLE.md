# Public Profile Copy Style

## Scope

This guide applies only to this academic website and the README in
[`trungnb/trungnb`](https://github.com/trungnb/trungnb). It does not govern CVs,
applications, manuscripts, or general writing preferences.

## Audience and voice

Write for prospective PhD supervisors and research collaborators. Lead with
CBCT, 3D imaging, and quantitative research; use clinical practice as relevant
context. Use direct, factual British English and natural Vietnamese.

## Format

- Keep bios to 60–90 words.
- Use one short overview and at most two bullets for an experience or project.
- Begin bullets with an active verb; state method, purpose, or result only when
  supported by the source material.
- Keep citations, links, dates, titles, front matter, and English–Vietnamese
  pairs intact.

## Accuracy

- Do not add claims, outcomes, tools, or roles not supported by the academic
  profile or project materials.
- Retain material boundaries: exploratory projects remain exploratory;
  PeriApicaI remains experimental, non-clinical, and not a medical device.
- Preserve formal publication citations verbatim and use official titles where
  applicable.
